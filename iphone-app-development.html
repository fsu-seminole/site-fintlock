iPhone app development / Tampa, Florida

A native iPhone app, built properly.

Fintlock designs and builds iPhone apps in Swift and SwiftUI, then carries them through TestFlight and App Store review. The whole app, not a website in a wrapper.

The problem

Why native, and why it matters

Most business owners come to us with the same worry: they have seen apps that feel slow, look borrowed, and get rejected by Apple, and they do not want to pay for one of those. The cause is usually the same. The app was built as a web page inside a shell, or by a team that had never shipped through App Store review.

A native app uses the phone the way Apple intended. It opens instantly, works offline where it should, uses the camera, notifications, and Face ID directly, and looks at home next to the apps your customers already trust. It is also what Apple's reviewers expect to see.

What we build

What an iPhone project includes

Every engagement covers the full path from idea to a listing on the App Store. Depending on the product, that means:

  • Product planning: working out the few screens that matter and cutting the rest
  • Interface design in Apple's own conventions, so the app feels familiar on day one
  • Native development in Swift and SwiftUI, with SwiftData or a backend where the product needs one
  • Subscriptions and one-time purchases through StoreKit, if the app is a product
  • Camera, location, notifications, and sign-in with Apple where they earn their place
  • TestFlight builds for your own testers before anything is public
  • The App Store listing, screenshots, privacy labels, and review submission
  • Updates for each autumn's iOS release, so the app keeps working

A good fit if

  • You have a product idea for consumers or professionals and want it done once, well
  • Your business needs a field app: capture, lookup, checklists, or photos from a job site
  • You already have an app that is slow, unmaintained, or stuck in review and want it rebuilt
  • You want a working build on your own phone every step of the way, not a slide deck about one

Questions

Things people ask about iPhone apps.

How long does an iPhone app take to build?

A focused first version usually takes a few months from kickoff to App Store submission. Complex products take longer. We tell you where yours lands after the first conversation, and the written scope fixes it.

What does an iPhone app cost?

It depends on what the app has to do, which is why we write the scope down first. You get a price before any code is written, and it does not move unless the scope does.

Do you build for Android too?

Our native work is iPhone. If you need both platforms, we will say so up front and talk through the options, including a browser app that works on any phone.

Who owns the app?

You do. The source code, the App Store listing, and the developer account are set up in your name.
