Fintlock builds web apps for businesses: customer portals, internal tools, and the integrations that move data between systems without anyone copying and pasting.

The problem

When the browser is the right place

Some software has to run everywhere: on the office desktop, on a phone at a job site, and on a customer's laptop at home. A web app needs no installation, is always the current version, and works on whatever device the person happens to have.

The catch is that a web app is only as good as the plumbing behind it. A portal that shows stale order status, or an internal tool that needs a spreadsheet export every morning, has not solved the problem. We build the connections first and the screens second.

What we build

What we build

Browser software with a proper backend, hosted on infrastructure in your name. Typical projects:

  • Customer portals: order status, documents, invoices, and requests in one place
  • Internal tools that replace the shared spreadsheet and let several people work at once
  • Integrations between accounting, CRM, inventory, and field systems
  • Dashboards that show the numbers people actually check, drawn from live data
  • Workflow automation: approvals, notifications, and hand-offs that used to travel by email
  • The web companion to an iPhone app, sharing the same accounts and data

A good fit if

  • Customers keep calling to ask about things they could look up themselves
  • Your team works from several locations or from the field
  • Two or more systems hold the same information and someone keeps them in sync by hand
  • You want software that updates in one place and never needs installing

Questions

Things people ask about web apps.

Where does the web app run?

On hosting set up in your name, sized to what the app needs. You pay the provider directly and hold the keys; we do the setup and keep it healthy.

Can it connect to our existing systems?

Almost always. Accounting packages, CRMs, inventory systems, and supplier portals expose data through APIs or exports, and connecting to them is a core part of the work.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Everything we build for the browser is designed for phones and tablets as well as desktops. If the phone experience needs the camera, offline use, or notifications, we may suggest a native iPhone app alongside it.

Who owns the code and the data?

You do. Source code, hosting accounts, and data are all yours from the start.
