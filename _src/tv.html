Why replace the stock screen

Your TV's home screen is built to sell. Yours shouldn't be.

The default smart TV home screen is designed around promotions: autoplaying trailers, sponsored rows, and apps you never asked for, often slow to respond. It's the first thing your family sees every time the TV turns on.

TV SmartHub is a home screen app made for one household. It opens first, shows only what you chose, and responds instantly to the remote.

TV SmartHub FAQ

Good questions.

Which TVs does TV SmartHub work with?

Samsung smart TVs that run Tizen, which covers most Samsung TVs from recent years. Our installs so far are on 2019 and 2020 models. Tell us your model number and we'll confirm before you commit. Ask us about other brands.

Will my TV still work normally?

Yes. Your custom home screen is an app that opens first. Every input, setting, and streaming app is still there, and the TV's own home screen is one button away.

Do you have to hack or jailbreak the TV?

No. We install through the TV maker's own developer tools, the same way app developers test their apps on a TV.

What do I need at home?

A supported smart TV on your home Wi-Fi. For the screen to open automatically every time the TV turns on, some setups also use a small always-on computer on the same network. We'll tell you whether yours needs one before we start.

Can it change later?

Yes. New apps, new teams, new holidays, or a whole new look. Send a message and we push the update to your TVs.

What does it cost, and where do you install?

After a short conversation about your household and your TVs, we quote a fixed price. Installs are done in person in the Tampa Bay area.
