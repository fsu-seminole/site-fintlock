Fintlock designs and builds custom apps for companies that need something specific: a product for customers, a tool for the team, or both, on iPhone and in the browser.

The problem

What custom means here

Plenty of companies can build an app. Fewer will sit with the people who do the work, understand the day-to-day operation, and build the app around it rather than around a template. That is the difference between software people use and software people work around.

Custom does not mean complicated. The best apps we build do a small number of things exactly right for one company. The scope is written down, the price is fixed against it, and you see working builds as it comes together rather than waiting for a reveal.

What we build

What a custom app project includes

We handle the whole thing in-house, from the first conversation to launch and after. A typical project covers:

  • A short discovery phase with the people who will use the app, not only the people paying for it
  • Interface design in the conventions of the platform, so nothing needs explaining
  • Native iPhone development in Swift, browser development with a proper backend, or both sharing one service
  • Accounts, payments, notifications, and integrations as the product needs them
  • Working builds on your own devices throughout, and a written scope that fixes the price
  • Launch through the App Store or your own hosting, then documentation and a clean handover

A good fit if

  • You are a business owner with a product idea and want one team that can design, build, and ship it
  • You need an app for your customers and a tool for your staff, and want them to share the same data
  • You have been quoted a big-agency price and a big-agency process and want something more direct
  • You would like to talk to the people writing the code, not an account manager

Questions

Things people ask about custom apps.

iPhone app or web app: which should we build?

If the app needs the camera, offline use, notifications, or a place on someone's home screen, native iPhone. If it needs to run on any device with nothing to install, the browser. Many products need both, sharing one backend, and we build those too.

How do you price a project?

Against a written scope. After the first conversation we send a scope and a price. The price holds unless the scope changes, and you decide when it does.

Do we need a detailed brief to start?

No. A few sentences about what happens today, who is involved, and what you want to change is enough. We do the rest of the discovery with you.

What happens after launch?

We can stay on for updates, new features, and the yearly round of operating system changes, or hand everything over to your team. The code and the accounts are yours either way.
