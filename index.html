FAQ

Good questions.

What does a project cost?

It depends on what we're building. After a first conversation we agree on a fixed scope and a fixed price, so you know the number before any work starts.

Who owns the code?

You do. You get the source, the documentation, and the accounts it runs on.

Which TVs work with TV SmartHub?

Samsung smart TVs that run Tizen, which covers most Samsung TVs from recent years. Tell us your model number and we'll confirm before you commit.

Will my TV still work normally?

Yes. Your custom home screen is an app that opens first. Every input, setting, and streaming app is still there, and the TV's own home screen is one button away.

Do you work outside Tampa?

For software, yes, anywhere in the US. TV home screen installs are done in person in the Tampa Bay area.

How long does it take?

We set a timeline together when we set the scope, and you see working builds along the way, so there's never a mystery about where things stand.

What could work better?

The process that eats your afternoons. The app you wish existed. The TV screen your kids keep landing on. Tell us about it.
